Session 5. Superconductivity, Nambu representation and Majorana physics

Learning outcomes

  • Derive the mean-field pairing term from an attractive Hubbard interaction, and why it breaks gauge symmetry
  • Write the Bogoliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian with the Nambu spinor, and read its electron and hole bands
  • Explain why an s-wave pairing opens a gap at the Fermi level regardless of the dispersion and of μ
  • Recognize s-wave, p-wave and d-wave, singlet and triplet, gapped and nodal superconductors
  • Relate the topology of the pairing to Majorana modes, at the edge in 2D and at the ends of the Kitaev chain
  • Explain how braiding Majorana zero modes gives a gate for topological quantum computing

Contents

  • Symmetry breaking and correlated matter in mean field
  • Macroscopic quantum phenomena, zero resistance and the Meissner effect
  • Attractive interactions and the pairing term
  • The Nambu spinor and the Bogoliubov-de Gennes Hamiltonian
  • Electron and hole bands, gapped and nodal superconductors
  • Unconventional pairing, spin-singlet and spin-triplet
  • Triplet superconductivity from a ferromagnet, s-wave, p-wave and d-wave
  • Same gap with a different topology
  • Majorana fermions and the Kitaev chain
  • Majorana platforms and topological quantum computing

Simulations shown

  • Self-consistent pairing Δ of the attractive Hubbard chain, growing with |U|
  • Bogoliubov-de Gennes bands of a single-orbital superconductor, gapped where electron and hole bands cross
  • s-wave and nodal d-wave superconductors on the square and triangular lattices, gap for every μ against nodes
  • Singlet and triplet pairing with disorder, the singlet gap surviving and the triplet gap closing
  • Triplet superconductor from a ferromagnet, gapped bulk and edge states with Chern number 1
  • s-wave against chiral p-wave on a ribbon, the same bulk gap but edge modes only for chiral p-wave
  • Open Kitaev chain, two Majorana zero modes at the ends, and the levels against μ closing the gap at |μ| = 2t
  • Two Kitaev wires, the degenerate ground states used for braiding
