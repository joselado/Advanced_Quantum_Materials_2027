Session 6. Classical magnetism and magnons

Learning outcomes

  • Explain how a repulsive Hubbard U breaks spin rotation symmetry in mean-field theory
  • Compute the exchange splitting and the antiferromagnetic gap of a self-consistent Hubbard chain
  • Use the Stoner criterion U D(E_F) > 1 to say which metals become magnetic
  • Derive the exchange J = 4t²/U of the Hubbard dimer with second-order perturbation theory
  • Recognize collinear order on bipartite lattices and 120 degree order on frustrated ones
  • Derive magnons as spin-1 bosons with the Holstein-Primakoff transformation

Contents

  • Symmetry-broken magnets and quantum magnets, magnons and spinons
  • Symmetry breaking by interactions, and where the interactions come from
  • The Hubbard model and its mean-field decoupling, collinear and non-collinear
  • Exchange splitting and an antiferromagnetic gap in a mean-field chain
  • Ferromagnet against antiferromagnet, and the critical interaction for magnetism
  • The strongly localized limit and the Hubbard dimer
  • J from second-order perturbation theory and the Heisenberg model on any lattice
  • Bipartite and frustrated lattices
  • Ferromagnetic coupling through a ligand and anisotropic exchange
  • Magnons, the Holstein-Primakoff transformation and topological magnons

Simulations shown

  • Mean-field Hubbard chain at filling 0.2, spin-split bands, exchange splitting 3.2t at U = 8t
  • Two-site Hubbard chain at half filling, antiferromagnetic gap U m opening at X
  • Energy of the ferromagnet minus the antiferromagnet against U, decaying as 4t²/U at large U
  • Self-consistent moment of a chain at filling 0.1 against U, zero below the Stoner threshold
  • Moment of the half-filled chain against U, going to one Bohr magneton at large U
  • Exact levels of the Hubbard dimer against U, singlet-triplet splitting J going to 4t²/U
  • Mean-field moments of square, honeycomb, kagome and triangular Hubbard models at U = 8t
  • Magnon bands of a honeycomb ferromagnet with spin-orbit coupling, Chern number and chiral edge magnons
