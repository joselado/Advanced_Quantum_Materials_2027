Advanced Quantum Materials 2027

The ten lectures of the course are here as two recorded versions with the same voice, and as written notes. Each session has its own page, with the learning outcomes, the contents and the simulations of the lecture, so the whole course can be studied from this site.

Video lectures

The prerecorded lectures with slides that follow the voice. Each step of a calculation is written when it is said. Next to the formulas there is a schematic or a plot, computed with the codes of the course. Each lecture has a second version, the landscape lecture, which is chosen above the video. It is lighter, with less content on the screen, and the physics is shown while we travel through 3D landscapes.

Lecture notes

The written version of the lectures, with the equations of the PDF. Each session is split in pages of one section, and the boxes point to the notebook of the session. The site can be downloaded as one zip and read on your computer without network. Only the videos and the live runs of the widgets need a connection.
