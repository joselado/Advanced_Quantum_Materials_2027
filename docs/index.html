Advanced Quantum Materials 2027

The ten lectures of the course are here in two versions, with the same recorded voice. The video lectures write every step of the calculations. The landscape lectures show the same physics with less on the screen. Each session has its own page, with the learning outcomes, the contents and the simulations of the lecture.

Video lectures

The prerecorded lectures with slides that follow the voice. Each step of a calculation is written when it is said. Next to the formulas there is a schematic or a plot, computed with the codes of the course.

  1. Second quantization, mean field and spontaneous symmetry breaking
  2. Symmetries, band structure theory and effective models
  3. Topological band structure theory
  4. Electrons in magnetic fields, the quantum Hall effect and Landau levels
  5. Superconductivity, Nambu representation and Majorana physics
  6. Classical magnetism and magnons
  7. Quantum entanglement and tensor networks
  8. Quantum magnetism, spinons and Kondo physics
  9. Excitations and defects in quantum materials
  10. Machine learning quantum materials

Landscape lectures

A lighter version of the same lectures, with the same voice. There is less content on the screen, and the physics is shown while we travel through 3D landscapes.

  1. Second quantization, mean field and spontaneous symmetry breaking
  2. Symmetries, band structure theory and effective models
  3. Topological band structure theory
  4. Electrons in magnetic fields, the quantum Hall effect and Landau levels
  5. Superconductivity, Nambu representation and Majorana physics
  6. Classical magnetism and magnons
  7. Quantum entanglement and tensor networks
  8. Quantum magnetism, spinons and Kondo physics
  9. Excitations and defects in quantum materials
  10. Machine learning quantum materials
