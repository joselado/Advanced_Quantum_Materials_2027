Session 3. Topological band structure theory

The video lecture of this session is not ready yet. The landscape lecture of this session is in the other tab.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain why perturbation theory fails for the ground state, even for a small impurity
  • Relate the Hall conductivity σ_xy to the Berry curvature and the Chern number, σ_xy = C e²/h
  • Explain the bulk-boundary correspondence, a gapped bulk with C gapless chiral modes at the edge
  • Compute the Zak phase and the end modes of the SSH chain
  • Recognize chiral and helical edge states, and the symmetry that protects the quantum spin Hall state
  • Recognize the same topological physics in photonic and mechanical systems

Contents

  • Band structures and the zoo of topological materials
  • Linear response
  • Where perturbation theory breaks down
  • Transverse conductivity and Berry curvature
  • Chern numbers and the chiral edge
  • The idea of topology and bulk-boundary correspondence
  • Three topological classes
  • The SSH chain and its Zak phase
  • Chiral and helical states, Chern insulators from a gapped Dirac cone
  • The quantum spin Hall state, and topology beyond electrons

Simulations shown

  • Density response of the square lattice to a local potential, oscillating with the Fermi wavelength
  • Overlap of the ground states of a chain with and without an impurity, going to zero with the size
  • Berry curvature of the Qi-Wu-Zhang model over the Brillouin zone, integrating to C = 1
  • Landau levels of the Hofstadter model, each with C = 1, and a ribbon with chiral edge states
  • Interface between a trivial and a C = 1 insulator, with a gapless mode around an impurity
  • Spectrum of the SSH chain, the two end modes at zero energy and the Zak phase in each phase
  • Quantum spin Hall ribbon with C↑ = +1 and C↓ = -1, the helical edge surviving Rashba coupling
  • Coupled photonic rings at flux 1/4 and a mechanical SSH chain of pendula, both with edge modes
