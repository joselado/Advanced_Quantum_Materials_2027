Session 8. Quantum magnetism, spinons and Kondo physics

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the difference between a classical magnet that breaks time reversal and a quantum magnet
  • Recognize magnons as spin-1 excitations and spinons as spin-1/2 excitations
  • Relate frustration on triangular and kagome lattices to quantum spin liquids
  • Use the parton transformation to write a spin liquid as a mean-field Hamiltonian of spinons
  • Explain the emergent gauge symmetry of the spinons and the vison as its defect
  • Explain how the Kondo hybridization opens a gap in a Kondo lattice

Contents

  • Classical and quantum magnets, and the materials that realize them
  • Magnons and spinons
  • The Ising and the Heisenberg dimer
  • Frustration, triangular and kagome lattices
  • Valence bond solid and resonating valence bond state
  • Why magnons fail, and the parton transformation
  • Spinon mean-field Hamiltonian and spinon dispersions
  • Emergent gauge symmetry and the vison
  • The Kondo impurity and the Kondo lattice
  • The heavy-fermion gap and the Kondo insulator

Simulations shown

  • Ising and Heisenberg dimers by exact diagonalization, singlet at -3J/4 below the triplet at J/4
  • Dimerized Heisenberg ring of 12 sites, triplon gap staying open as the coupling between dimers grows
  • Six-site ring, amplitudes of the singlet coverings and weight of the projected BCS state
  • Spinon bands for three choices of χ: gapless chain, gapped dimerized chain, Dirac honeycomb
  • Spinons on a 4 x 4 square lattice, levels unchanged by a gauge transformation, changed by a vison
  • Kondo impurity in a 3 x 3 electron cluster by exact diagonalization, singlet and screening cloud
  • Mean-field bands of a Kondo lattice chain, f level and conduction band anti-crossing into a gap
  • Indirect gap of the Kondo insulator against the hybridization γ_K
