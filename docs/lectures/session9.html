Session 9. Excitations and defects in quantum materials

Learning outcomes

  • Explain what a response function measures, a kick at one point and a measurement at another
  • Use the susceptibility above T_c to predict the order of a classical magnet
  • Compute the Lindhard response of a metal and relate its divergences to nesting and instabilities
  • Derive the random phase approximation and obtain the plasmon and the Stoner criterion from it
  • Relate the state bound to an impurity to the ground state: Friedel oscillations, Yu-Shiba-Rusinov states, topology
  • Explain Anderson localization and measure it with the inverse participation ratio

Contents

  • Responses in materials and the response function
  • The susceptibility of a classical magnet
  • Linear response, and where perturbation theory breaks down
  • The Fermi sea and the Lindhard response
  • Divergences, the dynamical response, the square lattice and nesting
  • The random phase approximation, plasmons and the Stoner criterion
  • Impurities in metals, Friedel oscillations
  • Magnetic impurities in superconductors, Yu-Shiba-Rusinov states
  • Vacancies in topological matter, and impurities in S = 1/2 and S = 1 chains
  • Quasi-periodicity, disorder and Anderson localization

Simulations shown

  • Density response of a tight-binding ring after a local kick, spreading in a light cone
  • Mean-field susceptibility of the Ising ferromagnet, paramagnet and antiferromagnet against temperature
  • Lindhard response of a chain and a square lattice, peak at 2k_F and divergence by nesting at half filling
  • Friedel oscillations around one impurity in a chain and in a square lattice
  • Yu-Shiba-Rusinov state of a magnetic impurity in an s-wave superconductor, crossing zero energy as J grows
  • In-gap states at a vacancy in a Chern insulator and in a p + ip superconductor
  • Moment around an impurity in Heisenberg chains with DMRG, power law for S = 1/2, exponential for S = 1
  • Inverse participation ratio of the Aubry-André chain, localization transition as the quasi-periodic potential grows
