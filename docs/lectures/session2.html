Session 2. Symmetries, band structure theory and effective models

The video lecture of this session is not ready yet. The landscape lecture of this session is in the other tab.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain why the eigenstates of H can be chosen as eigenstates of a symmetry that commutes with it
  • Derive Bloch's theorem from translational symmetry, with the quasi-momentum as the phase of the translation
  • Compute the band structure of single-orbital and multi-orbital tight-binding chains
  • Relate the density of states, group velocity, effective mass and Fermi surface to the band structure
  • Recognize parabolic, Dirac and flat dispersions in the square, honeycomb, Lieb and kagome lattices
  • Relate the band structure to what ARPES measures

Contents

  • Symmetries to simplify a quantum many-body problem
  • Symmetry operators, unitarity and their eigenvalues
  • Symmetries commuting with H, the rings of three and five sites
  • Translational symmetry and Bloch's theorem
  • The Brillouin zone, and bands beyond electrons
  • Single-orbital and multi-orbital band structures, the dimerized chain
  • Band structures in two and three dimensions
  • Density of states, group velocity, effective mass and Fermi surface
  • Parabolic, Dirac and flat dispersions in the honeycomb, Lieb and kagome lattices
  • Measuring band structures with ARPES

Simulations shown

  • Levels of rings of three, five and six sites, labeled by the eigenvalue of the rotation
  • Band 2t cos φ of the tight-binding chain, checked against a ring of 101 sites
  • Bands of a two-leg ladder, two cosines split by the rung hopping
  • Bands of a three-orbital chain and of the dimerized chain, with the gap at φ = π
  • Band structure of the square and cubic lattices, with the saddle points of the square lattice
  • Density of states of the chain and of the square lattice, with its log peak at E = 0
  • Honeycomb bands with the Dirac cones at K and K', and flat bands of the Lieb and kagome lattices
  • ARPES spectral function of the honeycomb lattice, with a broadening of 0.1t
