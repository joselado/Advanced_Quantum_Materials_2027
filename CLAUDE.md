# CLAUDE.md

Public teaching repo for Advanced Quantum Materials (Jose Lado, Aalto). Before writing or editing
any prose here (lecture notes in `lecture_notes/src/sessions/`, notebook markdown in
`jupyter-notebooks/`, slide text, narration), read `VOICE.md`: it describes the lecturer's voice
per register, with the transcripts in `transcripts/` (gitignored, local only) as primary evidence.

The prose of the lecture notes (`lecture_notes/src/sessions/*.tex` and the captions in
`lecture_notes/src/figures/*.tex`) follows `lecture_notes/writing_style.md`, which wins over
`VOICE.md` where the two disagree: simple English written by a non-native condensed matter
physicist, no colons, semicolons or em-dashes in prose.
