## Quantized Hall plateaus in experiment {#sec-4-6 number="4.6"}

When we sweep the magnetic field on a real 2DEG, we see this transition directly. At low field, before the quantum regime starts, the Hall resistance grows linearly with $B$, which is the classical Hall effect of ordinary electromagnetism. When the field reaches the tesla range, a staircase of sharp plateaus appears in the Hall resistance instead. The precise field scale depends on the material and follows the spacing of the Landau levels that we derive below. When the field increases further, the cyclotron energy $\hbar\omega_c$ grows, so fewer Landau levels stay below a fixed chemical potential. Each time the chemical potential goes past a Landau level, the number of filled levels $N$ in [(4.2)](#eq-4-2) decreases by one, and the Hall resistance jumps to the next quantized plateau.
