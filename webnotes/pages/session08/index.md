# Quantum Magnetism, Spinons and Kondo Physics {#session-8}

In Session 6 we only studied magnets that break time-reversal symmetry, which are ferromagnets and antiferromagnets. In the last part of that session we used the Holstein-Primakoff transformation to write these ordered states in terms of bosons, the magnons, and we went as far as a topological magnon band structure with its own chiral edge modes. That session stopped suddenly, at "topological magnons," without a final recap. It also left one open point. There was a short remark about Kitaev-type interactions, which were called "important for quantum spin liquids" at that time, but we did not continue with them. In this session we come back to that point, as a lecture of its own, with its own introduction and its own plan. First, we will see minimal models of quantum magnetism. Then we will see the excitations that quantum spin liquids (QSLs) have, which are spinons and gauge excitations. At the end we will study Kondo systems and Kondo lattice models, which are a very different physical system that can be solved with the same method. The idea that connects the whole session is the same idea that we have used in this course since Session 1. We take a many-body Hamiltonian that mean-field theory cannot treat, we write it with the right auxiliary particles, and we do mean-field theory on *that* problem.

In this session we use $\hbar = 1$ for the spin operators, which is the same convention that we already used without saying it in Session 6.
