% Session 8 -- the parton construction: an enlarged local Hilbert space, cut back down
% by the single-occupancy constraint, leaving a tight-binding model for spinons.
% Placement: sec. "The parton construction", after the single-occupancy constraint (8.11).
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s08:parton}{What the Abrikosov-fermion rewriting (8.10) costs, and what it
buys. (a) A site's four fermionic states. The two singly-occupied ones reproduce the physical
spin-$1/2$ doublet exactly; the empty and doubly-occupied ones are extra, unphysical states that
(8.10) maps to $\mathbf S_i=\mathbf 0$ and that the constraint (8.11) -- the operator-side twin of
the Gutzwiller projector (8.7) -- removes by hand. (b) What is left after the mean-field decoupling
(8.14): an ordinary tight-binding problem (8.15) for charge-neutral, spin-$1/2$ fermions hopping
with the self-consistent bond amplitude $\chi_{ij}$. One fermion per site is the reason the
resulting spinon model must be studied at half filling, and choosing the same $\chi_{ij}$ for both
spin species keeps $\langle\mathbf S_i\rangle=\mathbf 0$, so unlike the magnetic mean fields of
sessions 1 and 6 this one breaks no time-reversal symmetry.}
