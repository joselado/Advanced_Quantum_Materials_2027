% Session 2 -- three archetypes of dispersion and the density of states each produces.
% Placement: sec. "Three archetypes of dispersion", alongside the enumerated list.
%
% DIMENSION: every panel is TWO-dimensional, and the caption says so. This is not
% cosmetic -- g(omega) ~ |omega| for a Dirac point is 2D-specific, and the parabolic
% 2D density of states is a step. Mixing dimensions here would contradict the 1D
% density of states drawn in figure fig:s02:cosband, which diverges at the band edges.
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s02:archetypes}{The three types of dispersion and the densities of states that
they give, all in \emph{two} dimensions. A parabolic band gives a constant density of states, which
is a step. A Dirac point makes it vanish linearly, so a finite interaction strength is needed before
anything happens. A flat band makes it diverge, so an arbitrarily weak interaction can drive the
symmetry-broken states of Session 1. Almost every band structure in this course is locally one of
these three.}

\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=1cm]

  xtick=\empty, ytick=\empty, axis lines=middle]

\end{scope}

\begin{scope}[shift={(5.0,0)}]

  \addplot[aqmcurve, domain=0:1.15, samples=2] {x};

\end{scope}
