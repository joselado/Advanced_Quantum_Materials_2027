\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s07:jwstring}{Spin equals fermion times string. On a single site the mapping
is trivial, since an empty site is a down spin and an occupied site is an up spin, $S^z_i = n_i -
\tfrac12$, as the ball and the arrow on each site show. The naive identification gives the wrong
\emph{relative} signs between sites, because fermion operators on different sites anticommute while
spin operators on different sites commute. The string $K_i$ of (7.2) corrects exactly this, since it
gives a factor $-1$ for every fermion to the left of site $i$, which is the shaded strip. The two
chains in the figure differ only in the occupations to the left of the marked site $i=6$, with three
fermions in the chain behind and two in the chain in front, and this parity alone changes the sign
of $S_6^+ = c_6^\dagger K_6$. The string is nonlocal, and it carries the fermionic exchange sign
that spins do not have by themselves.}
