% Session 4 -- why Landau levels are flat: the Landau-gauge reduction turns every
% k_y into the same harmonic oscillator, only displaced.
% Placement: sec. "Landau levels of Schroedinger electrons", after equation (4.6).
% Rendered by figures/blender/s04f03_guidingcenter.py, labels in figures/overlays/s04f03_guidingcenter.tex.
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s04:guidingcenter}{Why the Landau levels are flat. (a) In the Landau gauge
each $k_y$ reduces (4.5) to a one-dimensional harmonic oscillator in $x$, which is the same in every
respect except that it is centred on the guiding centre $x_0=\hbar k_y/B$. When $k_y$ increases, the
whole well moves to the right without changing its curvature. Every well therefore has the same
ladder $E_n=\hbar\omega_c(n+\tfrac12)$, drawn here as the horizontal guides, and the ground-state
wavepacket of each well, the red bump, has the same shape at a different position. (b) As a function
of $k_y$, this is a set of bands with exactly no dispersion, and the three balls are the three
values of $k_y$ of (a). The flatness is not an artefact of the gauge. It means that the energy does
not depend on the position of the electron in the sample, and this is also the origin of the very
large degeneracy of the Landau levels.}

\end{aqmfigure}
