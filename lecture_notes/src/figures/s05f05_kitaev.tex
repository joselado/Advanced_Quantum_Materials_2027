% Session 5 -- the Kitaev chain: which Majoranas pair with which.
% Placement: sec. "The Kitaev chain", after the SSH-recognition paragraph.
%
% Numbering follows the text exactly: site n carries gamma_{2n-1} and gamma_{2n}, so the
% chain of N sites carries gamma_1 ... gamma_{2N}. Drawn for N = 5, hence gamma_{2N} = gamma_10.
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s05:kitaev}{The Kitaev chain in terms of Majorana variables, for $N=5$ sites
(so $\gamma_{2N}=\gamma_{10}$). Splitting every electron into its two Majorana halves (5.17) has no
physical content by itself, and what matters is which halves the Hamiltonian binds together. In the
trivial phase, at the top, the binding is inside each site. The two halves of each electron stay
bound to each other, as they were in the vacuum, and every mode costs energy. In the topological
phase, at the bottom, the binding is between sites, $\gamma_{2n}$ with $\gamma_{2n+1}$, and the
pattern has one bond missing at each end. So $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_{2N}$ do not appear in (5.21),
cost no energy and are at opposite ends of the sample. The ordinary fermion that they would form
together has been split into two halves, which are now separated by the length of the chain, and
this is what \emph{deconfined} means here. This is the fully dimerized SSH chain of the
topological-band-structure session with its two remaining end sites, with new labels, so the
counting is the same and only the operators are new.}
