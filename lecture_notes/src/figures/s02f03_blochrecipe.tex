\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s02:recipe}{Reading a Bloch Hamiltonian from a lattice, shown as band folding.
The number of orbitals per unit cell gives the size of the matrix. An intracell hopping enters the
entry of its orbital pair with no phase, and an intercell hopping enters the corresponding entry
multiplied by $e^{\pm i\varphi}$. (a) The same chain with one site per cell, behind, and with two
sites $A$, $B$ per cell, in front, where the intracell hopping $1$ is the thick bond and the
intercell hopping $\eta$ is the thin one. Note that the intracell $A$-$B$ hopping goes \emph{off}
the diagonal. For the chain with alternating hoppings (2.13) this gives the two bands
$\epsilon_\pm=\pm|1+\eta e^{-i\varphi}|$. (b) For $\eta=1$ the two descriptions are the same chain,
and the band of the one-site cell, the grey tube, folds into the zone of the two-site cell, which is
half as wide and ends at the translucent planes at $\varphi=\pm\pi$. The two folded branches are the
two bands, which touch at the zone edge. For $\eta=0.6$ they have a gap, and the gap closes exactly
at $\eta=1$. This is the Su-Schrieffer-Heeger chain of the topology session.}
