% Session 6 -- the Kitaev interaction: frustration that lives in the coupling, not in the lattice.
% Placement: sec. "Anisotropic exchange", in the Kitaev paragraph, after the H_Kitaev display.
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s06:kitaev}{The Kitaev interaction and the second kind of frustration. The
honeycomb lattice is bipartite: its sites two-colour (filled/open) exactly as in
figure~\ref{fig:s06:frustration}(a), so an ordinary Heisenberg antiferromagnet on it is not
frustrated at all. The Kitaev coupling frustrates it anyway, because each of the three bond
directions couples only \emph{one} spin component: the $x$-bond cares only about $S^x$ and so wants
both of its spins along $\pm\hat x$, the $y$-bond wants $\pm\hat y$, the $z$-bond wants
$\pm\hat z$. Every site sits on one bond of each type, and no single orientation can satisfy three
mutually orthogonal demands at once. This is frustration built into the interaction rather than
into the geometry, and it is what makes the Kitaev model a paradigm for quantum spin liquids in
sessions 7 and 8.}
