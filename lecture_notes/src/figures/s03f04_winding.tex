\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s03:winding}{A drawing of the one-dimensional invariant. On the left of each
panel is the off-diagonal element $d(k)=t+e^{ik}$ of the SSH Bloch Hamiltonian (3.5) in the complex
plane, as $k$ goes once through the Brillouin zone, and the arrow shows the direction of increasing
$k$. It is always the unit circle, but centred at $(t,0)$, so it encloses the origin, the ball, for
$t<1$ and does not enclose it for $t>1$. The winding number of this curve around the origin is
exactly $\gamma/\pi$, which is the Zak phase (3.6) in units of $\pi$. On the right of each panel are
the corresponding bands $E_\pm(k)=\pm|d(k)|$, with gap $2|1-t|$. The two halves give the same
information in two ways. The only way to unwind the curve is to move it across the origin, and
$d(k)=0$ is where the gap closes, so the invariant cannot change while the system stays insulating.
Note that (a) and (c) have the same gap and look like ordinary spectra. The difference between them
cannot be seen in the energies and is only in the wavefunctions, which is the main point of (3.4).
By the bulk-boundary correspondence, $\gamma=\pi$ is also exactly the condition for the zero-energy
end modes of figure~\ref{fig:s03:ssh}.}

\end{aqmfigure}
