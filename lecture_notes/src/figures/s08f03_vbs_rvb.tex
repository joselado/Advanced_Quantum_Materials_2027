% Session 8 -- valence bond solid versus resonating valence bond: a fixed dimer covering
% with confined triplons, against a superposition of coverings with deconfined spinons.
% Placement: end of sec. "The QSL ground state: the resonating valence bond wavefunction",
% referenced from the dimerized-Heisenberg section as well.
%
% NOTE (physics): drawn on a chain throughout, so that the only difference between the two
% ground states is the one that matters -- one covering versus a superposition of them.
% The confining string in (a2) is the set of strong (J) bonds left without their singlet;
% one more of them is damaged for every site the two unpaired spins move apart, which is
% what binds them into a single S=1 triplon.
\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s08:vbsrvb}{The difference between a valence bond solid and a quantum spin
liquid is not whether the ground state is built from singlets, since both are, but whether the
singlet pattern is frozen. (a1) The fully dimerized model (8.4) has one fixed covering, (8.5).
(a2) When we separate the two unpaired spins of figure~\ref{fig:s08:magnonspinon}b, every strong
bond between them loses its singlet, so the energy cost grows with the separation. The pair is
confined, and only the bound object propagates, which is the spin-$1$ triplon. (b1) The RVB state
(8.7) is a superposition over \emph{all} pairings, near and far. (b2) Now the background pairs
again behind the excitations with no cost, the confining string does not form, and the two halves
separate as independent spin-$1/2$ spinons. Frustration is the reason why the superposition in
(b1), and not a single covering, is the ground state.}

  {thick $=$ the strong bonds $J$ of (8.4):\\[1pt]

% --- a2: two unpaired spins, joined by a string ------------------------------

  \draw[capsule] (\n*\dx-0.17,-1.48) rectangle (\n*\dx+\dx+0.17,-1.12);
}

  \draw[capsulebad] (\n*\dx-0.17,-1.48) rectangle (\n*\dx+\dx+0.17,-1.12);

\foreach \n in {0,2,4,6,8,10} { \draw[aqmbondstrong] (\n*\dx,-1.30) -- (\n*\dx+\dx,-1.30); }

  \node[aqmlabsmall, text=aqmbroken, fill=white, inner sep=0.6pt]

\draw[aqmbrace, decoration={brace, amplitude=3.5pt, mirror}]
  (2*\dx,-1.62) -- (7*\dx,-1.62);
\node[aqmlabsmall, anchor=north, align=center, text=aqmbroken] at (4.5*\dx,-1.80)
  {string: one more strong bond spoiled\\[-1pt]for every site of separation};
\node[aqmlabsmall, anchor=east, text=aqmmuted, align=right] at (\xa,-1.30)
  {(a2) break one\\[-1pt]singlet};
\node[aqmlabsmall, anchor=west, align=left] at (\xr,-1.30)
  {cost $\propto$ separation: the two halves stay\\[1pt]
   bound $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{triplon}, $\Delta S = 1$};

% ============================ (b) RVB ========================================
\node[aqmpanel, anchor=west] at (\xa-2.65,-2.75) {(b) resonating valence bond};

% --- b1: the superposition over coverings ------------------------------------

% --- b2: deconfined spinons --------------------------------------------------
