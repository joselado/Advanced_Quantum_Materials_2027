\begin{aqmfigure}{fig:s07:merapeps}{The two tensor networks beyond the chain, and what their
geometry gives. Left, the multi-scale entanglement renormalization ansatz. Its tensors are stacked
in layers. Each layer first removes the short-range entanglement between neighbouring blocks with
the disentanglers, the boxes, and then it combines two sites into one with the triangular
isometries, so the network describes the state at longer and longer length scales and not at
successive positions along a chain. A division of the system, the red plane, now cuts one bond in
every layer instead of one bond in total, and this is how MERA can describe the logarithmically
growing entropy (7.12) of a critical chain, which an MPS with fixed bond dimension cannot reach.
Right, projected entangled pair states, the direct 2D generalization of (7.5), with one tensor per
lattice site that has a physical leg and four virtual bonds. The figure also shows the difficulty.
There is no way to sweep along such a network one tensor at a time with the rest reduced to a simple
environment, and the exact contraction of the network is itself a hard problem, which is why PEPS
have no simple analogue of DMRG.}
